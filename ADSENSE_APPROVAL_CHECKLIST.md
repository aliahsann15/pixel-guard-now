## 📋 Pre-Approval Checklist

Before submitting to AdSense, verify:

### Domain & Hosting
- [ ] Site is live at https://pixelguard.site
- [ ] Domain is at least 6 months old (if required by AdSense)
- [ ] SSL certificate is active and valid
- [ ] Site loads quickly (< 3 seconds)

### Content Review
- [ ] All pages have original, valuable content
- [ ] No duplicate content from other websites
- [ ] Grammar and spelling are correct
- [ ] Images have proper alt tags
- [ ] No broken links or 404 errors

### AdSense Setup
- [ ] Google AdSense account created
- [ ] Site added to AdSense dashboard
- [ ] Site ownership verified
- [ ] Publisher ID correctly placed in code
- [ ] Ad units created in AdSense dashboard
- [ ] Real ad slot IDs replace placeholder IDs

### Policy Compliance
- [ ] No prohibited content
- [ ] No copyright violations
- [ ] Privacy policy mentions data collection
- [ ] Terms of service in place
- [ ] Contact information clearly visible

### Traffic & Analytics
- [ ] Google Analytics or similar installed (optional but recommended)
- [ ] Site is indexed by Google (check: site:pixelguard.site)
- [ ] Site receives regular organic traffic
- [ ] Content is being shared/linked to
